AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER

by and among

HALCYON ROBOTICS, INC.,

HALCYON MERGER SUB, INC.

and

BLUEFIN DATA SYSTEMS, INC.

Dated as of March 2, 2026

This AGREEMENT AND PLAN OF MERGER (this “Agreement"), dated as of March 2,
2026, is entered into by and among Halcyon Robotics, Inc., a Delaware
corporation ("Parent"), Halcyon Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a
wholly owned subsidiary of Parent ("Merger Sub"), and Bluefin Data Systems,
Inc., a Delaware corporation (the "Company"). This sample agreement is
entirely fictional and was written to test document analysis software.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the respective boards of directors of Parent, Merger Sub and the
Company have each approved the merger of Merger Sub with and into the Company
(the "Merger"), with the Company surviving the Merger as a wholly owned
subsidiary of Parent (the "Surviving Corporation"), upon the terms and subject
to the conditions set forth in this Agreement;

WHEREAS, the board of directors of the Company has determined that the Merger
is fair to, and in the best interests of, the Company and its stockholders and
has resolved to recommend that the stockholders of the Company adopt this
Agreement;

WHEREAS, concurrently with the execution of this Agreement, Parent has
delivered to the Company a debt commitment letter from Meridian Capital
Partners pursuant to which the lenders have committed to provide a senior
secured term loan facility in an aggregate principal amount of $350 million
(the "Debt Financing"); and

WHEREAS, Parent, Merger Sub and the Company desire to make certain
representations, warranties, covenants and agreements in connection with the
Merger.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing and the mutual covenants
contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

DEFINITIONS

Section 1.1 Certain Definitions. For purposes of this Agreement:

"Annual Recurring Revenue" or "ARR" means, as of any date, the annualized
value of all subscription contracts of the Company, including (i) contracts
that have been executed but under which services have not yet commenced, (ii)
pilot, trial and proof-of-concept arrangements, whether or not paid, and (iii)
contracts subject to termination for convenience by the customer on less than
thirty (30) days' notice, in each case calculated by multiplying the most
recent monthly contract value by twelve (12).

"Business Day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday or a day on which
banks in New York, New York are authorized or required by law to close.

"Company Material Adverse Effect" means any event, change, effect, development
or occurrence that, individually or in the aggregate, has had a material
adverse effect on the business, results of operations or financial condition
of the Company and its Subsidiaries, taken as a whole; provided, however, that
none of the following shall be deemed to constitute, or be taken into account
in determining whether there has been, a Company Material Adverse Effect: (a)
changes in general economic, political or financial market conditions; (b)
changes generally affecting the industries in which the Company operates; (c)
changes in law or GAAP; (d) any pandemic, epidemic, natural disaster, act of
war or terrorism; (e) any failure by the Company to meet any internal or
published projections, forecasts, budgets or revenue or earnings predictions;
(f) the loss of, or any adverse change in the relationship with, any customer,
supplier, employee or partner of the Company, including any of the Company's
ten largest customers; (g) any cybersecurity incident, data breach or
ransomware attack affecting the Company's systems; (h) any action taken by the
Company that is required or permitted by this Agreement; (i) any decline in
the market price or trading volume of the Company Common Stock; and (j) any
litigation arising from or relating to this Agreement or the Merger. For the
avoidance of doubt, the exceptions in clauses (a) through (j) shall apply
whether or not such matters disproportionately affect the Company relative to
other participants in the industries in which the Company operates.

"Indebtedness" means all obligations of the Company for borrowed money,
including all amounts outstanding under the Credit Agreement dated as of June
14, 2023 among the Company, Northgate Bank, N.A., as administrative agent, and
the lenders party thereto (the "Company Credit Agreement").

"Knowledge" means, with respect to the Company, the actual knowledge, without
any duty of inquiry, of the Chief Executive Officer of the Company.

"Outside Date" means December 2, 2026; provided that if on such date all
conditions other than the Regulatory Conditions have been satisfied, the
Outside Date shall automatically be extended to March 2, 2027.

ARTICLE II
THE MERGER; MERGER CONSIDERATION

Section 2.1 The Merger. Upon the terms and subject to the conditions of this
Agreement and in accordance with the Delaware General Corporation Law, at the
Effective Time, Merger Sub shall be merged with and into the Company, the
separate corporate existence of Merger Sub shall cease, and the Company shall
continue as the Surviving Corporation.

Section 2.2 Merger Consideration. At the Effective Time, each share of common
stock of the Company issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective
Time shall be converted into the right to receive (a) $14.95 in cash, without
interest (the "Cash Consideration"), and (b) 0.1150 shares of common stock of
Parent (the "Stock Consideration" and, together with the Cash Consideration,
the "Merger Consideration"). Based on the closing price of Parent common stock
on the last trading day prior to the date hereof, the Merger Consideration
represents an implied value of $18.40 per share and an aggregate equity value
of approximately $640 million, consisting of approximately $520 million in
cash and approximately $120 million in Parent common stock.

Section 2.3 Escrow. At the Closing, Parent shall deposit $25 million of the
aggregate Cash Consideration (the "Escrow Amount") with Atlas Trust Company,
as escrow agent, to secure the indemnification obligations set forth in
Article IX. The Escrow Amount, less any amounts subject to pending claims,
shall be released to the former stockholders of the Company on the date that
is twelve (12) months following the Closing Date.

Section 2.4 Earnout. In addition to the Merger Consideration, Parent shall pay
to the former holders of Company Common Stock an aggregate amount of up to $60
million (the "Earnout Payment") if Adjusted Revenue for the twenty-four (24)
month period following the Closing Date equals or exceeds $460 million.
"Adjusted Revenue" means revenue of the Surviving Corporation as calculated by
the Stockholder Representative in good faith, including revenue attributable
to any products of Parent sold to customers of the Company. If Parent sells,
transfers or undergoes a change of control, or materially changes the go-to-
market strategy of the Surviving Corporation, prior to the end of such period,
the full Earnout Payment shall become immediately due and payable regardless
of Adjusted Revenue.

Section 2.5 Closing. The closing of the Merger (the "Closing") shall take
place on the third Business Day following the satisfaction or waiver of the
conditions set forth in Article VII. The parties currently anticipate that the
Closing will occur within approximately six (6) months following the date of
this Agreement.

ARTICLE IV
REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES OF PARENT AND MERGER SUB

Section 4.1 Organization. Each of Parent and Merger Sub is a corporation duly
incorporated, validly existing and in good standing under the laws of the
State of Delaware.

Section 4.5 Financing. Parent has delivered to the Company a true and complete
copy of the debt commitment letter. The aggregate proceeds of the Debt

consummate the Merger, are not subject to any condition regarding Parent's or

Section 5.3 No Solicitation. The Company shall not solicit or knowingly

ARTICLE VI
ADDITIONAL AGREEMENTS

Section 6.2 Transaction Expenses. Each party shall bear its own fees and
expenses incurred in connection with this Agreement, except that Parent shall
bear all filing fees under the HSR Act and all fees of the escrow agent.
Parent's aggregate transaction expenses, including advisory, legal, financing
and filing fees, are estimated at approximately $21 million.

Section 6.4 Indemnification of Directors and Officers. For six years after the
Effective Time, Parent shall cause the Surviving Corporation to indemnify each
current and former director and officer of the Company and shall maintain
directors' and officers' liability insurance with coverage no less favorable
than existing coverage, at an annual premium of up to 300% of the current
annual premium.

Section 7.1 Conditions to Each Party's Obligation. The obligations of each
party to effect the Merger are subject to (a) adoption of this Agreement by
the Company stockholders; (b) expiration or termination of the waiting period
under the HSR Act and receipt of approval from the European Commission (the
"Regulatory Conditions"); and (c) the absence of any order enjoining the
Merger.

Section 7.2 Conditions to Obligations of Parent. The obligations of Parent to
effect the Merger are further subject to (a) the representations and
warranties of the Company being true and correct, except where the failure to
be true and correct would not have a Company Material Adverse Effect; and (b)
the Company having performed in all material respects its covenants.

ARTICLE VIII
TERMINATION

Section 8.1 Termination. This Agreement may be terminated at any time prior to
the Effective Time (a) by mutual written consent; (b) by either party if the
Closing has not occurred by the Outside Date; (c) by either party if a final
non-appealable order permanently enjoins the Merger; (d) by the Company in
order to accept a Superior Proposal; or (e) by Parent if the Company board
changes its recommendation.

Section 8.2 Company Termination Fee. If this Agreement is terminated pursuant
to Section 8.1(d) or Section 8.1(e), the Company shall pay to Parent a fee of
$12 million (the "Company Termination Fee").

Section 8.3 Parent Termination Fee. If this Agreement is terminated (a)
pursuant to Section 8.1(b) or Section 8.1(c) at a time when any of the
Regulatory Conditions has not been satisfied, or (b) by the Company because
Parent fails to consummate the Closing when required, including as a result of
any failure of the Debt Financing, Parent shall pay to the Company a fee of
$48 million in cash (the "Parent Termination Fee") within two Business Days.
The Parent Termination Fee shall not be the Company's sole and exclusive
remedy, and the Company shall also be entitled to seek specific performance
and damages, including damages based on the loss of the economic benefits of
the Merger to the Company's stockholders.

Halcyon Robotics to Acquire Bluefin Data Systems for $640 Million. Halcyon

approximately $38 million by the end of the second year following closing,
primarily from consolidating cloud infrastructure, facilities and go-to-market
overlap, and annual revenue synergies of approximately $30 million from cross-
selling Bluefin's analytics platform into Halcyon's installed base. Halcyon
expects the transaction to be accretive to adjusted earnings in the first full
year after closing. Halcyon reported revenue of $1.31 billion and operating
expenses of $1.12 billion for fiscal 2025 and ended the year with $410 million
of cash and cash equivalents.
